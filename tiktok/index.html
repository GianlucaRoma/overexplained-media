The Overexplained Publisher

A private publishing tool used only by the creator of @theoverexplained (and its sister account @plantsoverexplained) to publish their own original animated science videos to their own TikTok accounts.

The creator makes every video, writes every caption and decides when each one is published. The tool uploads the creator's videos to the creator's own account, with the privacy and interaction settings the creator chooses. It does not access other users' accounts or data.
