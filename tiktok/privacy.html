Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

The Overexplained Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by the creator of @theoverexplained to publish their own videos to their own TikTok accounts.

What data the app uses

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the connected account (display name, avatar, open ID), only to show which account is connected.
  • An access token issued by TikTok after the account owner authorizes the app, used only to upload and publish the owner's videos.
  • The videos and captions created by the account owner.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not sell, share or rent any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored encrypted in the owner's private cloud environment and are deleted when access is revoked. The account owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

Contact

Questions: contact @theoverexplained on Instagram or TikTok.
